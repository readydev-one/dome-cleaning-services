What Impacts the Cost of Cleaning Services in Edmonton?

Cleaning prices are not one-size-fits-all. Several key factors determine the final cost. The more detailed the service, the higher the price.

  • 1. Size of the Space – Detect where prospects drop off and optimize those areas.

  • 2. Type of Cleaning Service –
    Standard cleaning covers surface-level tasks: Dusting and vacuuming, Kitchen and bathroom cleaning, Floors and surfaces
    Deep cleaning includes baseboards, inside appliances, and buildup removal
    Move-out cleaning focuses on rental turnover and inspections : Inside cabinets and appliances, Walls, doors, and trim, Bathroom sanitization
    Post-construction cleaning removes dust, debris, and residue

  • 3. Condition of the Property -
    A well-maintained home costs less to clean than one that hasn’t been cleaned in months. If cleaners need to deal with:
    - Heavy grease
    - Pet hair
    - Soap scum or hard water stains
    Expect higher pricing due to added labor time.
